# Personal Screen-Time Control

The app's working name is **Morrow**.

This context defines the product language for an Android-first app that helps people limit their own use of distracting apps, with iOS as a later platform.

## Language

**Selected app**: An app a person chooses to limit because they spend more time in it than they intend.

**App block**: A rule that prevents a selected app from opening while the rule is active.

**Daily allowance**: The maximum time a person chooses to spend in a selected app during a local calendar day.

**Launch interruption**: A prompt that interrupts opening a selected app so the person can reconsider before proceeding.

Selected apps have a 15-second launch delay. Their daily allowance starts after that delay; exhausted apps remain covered until local midnight.
